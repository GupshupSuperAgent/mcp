# Roadmap

Gupshup serves three kinds of customers. Enterprise and self-serve are available today; CX is next.

| Offering | For | Channels | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enterprise MCP** | Enterprise customers managed by a Gupshup account team, using Gupshup Enterprise APIs | WhatsApp, SMS, RCS | ✅ Available (beta) |
| **Self-serve MCP** | Developers on the [Gupshup messaging API](https://docs.gupshup.io/reference/msg) | WhatsApp, SMS, RCS, Truecaller | ✅ Available at `https://docs.gupshup.io/mcp` ([setup](self-serve.md)) |
| **CX MCP** | Teams on the Gupshup CX platform: journeys, AI agents, human handoff, agent assist | Omnichannel | 🔜 Coming soon |

For early access to the CX server, email **[sales@gupshup.ai](mailto:sales@gupshup.ai)** with your use case.

Scope and timing for anything marked *Coming soon* may change. This page doesn't commit to specific tools or dates. Follow this repository (**Watch → Custom → Releases**) to hear when new servers ship.
